FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does setup take? +

Most teams connect their helpdesk and docs and are drafting replies within a few hours. There's no migration step — Harbor reads what you already have.

Which helpdesks does Harbor work with? +

Harbor connects to the most common helpdesk and messaging tools out of the box, and supports a general API for anything custom.

Does Harbor ever send replies without a human? +

Only for the routine, low-risk replies you choose to automate. Anything flagged as sensitive or uncertain always waits for a person to approve it.

Can I switch plans later? +

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your billing settings — changes apply from your next billing cycle.

Is my customer data secure? +

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Enterprise plans support custom data residency and retention policies.
